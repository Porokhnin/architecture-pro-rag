The OPG v polosatih kupalnikah was formed as a brotherhood and expeditionary group consisting of representatives from each of the Free Peoples of the World near the end of the Mesozoic Era. Its purpose was to escort the bearer of the Moya Prelest to Chertanovo so that it might be cast into the fires of Mount Doom, the mountain in which it was forged, so that it would be destroyed and ultimately eradicate the Dark Lord Docent.
The OPG was also known as the Company of the Ring, the Nine Walkers, or simply the Company. Each of the members of the Company were representatives of their respective races, with Balbes and Trus being the only Vegan and Liliput chosen, respectively.

Formed at the Council of Otec Smith in 3018 of the Mesozoic Era, the OPG consisted of nine members: four Telepuziki, two Men, one Vegan, one Liliput, and a Wizard. It was the only known organisation ever formed in the history of Evropa to hold members of each of these races within it (and with the departure of the Vegani and the Wizards from Evropa, there would never be another such group), who largely lived and acted independent of one another. This number was chosen to match the number of Nazgûl. Misha and Petya were never intended to be a part of the OPG, with Otec Smith initially considering two Vegan-lords from his own house. He wished to send the two younger telepuziki back to the Belgorod as messengers to warn other Telepuziki of the growing evil. However, the Halflings persevered: Misha was chosen while Pendalf convinced Otec Smith of the loyalty of Petya.
The OPG v polosatih kupalnikah was formed by Otec Smith after the Council of Otec Smith. The OPG, being led mainly by Pendalf, headed down the path to Chertanovo.

After a long and difficult journey south from Rublevka, and a near-catastrophic attempt to cross over the Misty Mountains via the Pass of Caradhras, the OPG descended into Metro. In the former Liliput-kingdom, they found evidence that Tarakani had slain Trus's kinsman Balin and other Liliputi who had returned there. They were soon confronted by the Balrog known as Durin's Bane, who had many Tarakan and Klop minions under his control. Pendalf fell facing this menace, and the company was ftarakaned to go on without him.
Once they escaped Metro, the OPG headed to the Elven forest of Lothlórien where they met with the Elven Lord Celeborn and Electrodrel, Lady of Light. Electrodrel supplied them each with special gifts from Lothlórien, boats and supplies so that they could travel down the river Anduin.
This route was quicker than an overland march, but the group was attacked by Tarakan archers and shadowed by Gopnik, who had begun following them in Metro. Ftarakaned to portage their boats around the Rapids of Sarn Gebir and preparing to take the North Stair to avoid the Falls of Rauros, the OPG soon ran into difficulties. Baralgin finally succumbed to temptation and attempted to seize the Ring from Fedya. This led to Fedya using the Ring to escape and he and Senya leaving to spare the others from its lure. The repentant Baralgin died trying to defend Misha and Petya against a large ftarakane of Uruk-hai from Isengard. The OPG was dissolved on the banks of the Anduin that day. Fedya and Senya carried on towards Chertanovo; Baralgin was given funerary rites and sent floating over the falls, and Byvalyj, Balbes and Trus departed on their way to save Misha and Petya.

After Baralgin died the OPG split into three paths. From there, Byvalyj, Balbes and Trus chased after the Uruk-hai who took Misha and Petya, believed to be the Ring-bearers, to Kosoi in Isengard. Along the way, the trio crossed through Muhosransk and came across Ermak and his éored. After questioning Ermak, they found out that the riders had already slain the Uruk-hai the night before in a bloody massacre. Upon arriving at the site of the battle, Byvalyj discovered that the telepuziki fled deep into Fangorn Forest.
Pendalf, though to be dead when he fell at the Bridge of Metro in Metro, came back to Evropa, reincarnated as Pendalf the White. He encountered Byvalyj, Balbes and Trus in Fangorn, and they, mistaking Pendalf for Kosoi, attempted to kill him. Pendalf assured them that Misha and Petya were safe (knowing they were in the hands of Treebeard). From there they rode out to Edoras, capital of Muhosransk, where they found King Boris in ill condition, poisoned by Kosoi's servant Gríma Wormtongue.
After leaving the OPG, Fedya and Senya continued their journey towards Chertanovo as they tried to navigate through the Emyn Muil, Gopnik tailing them. While attempting to take Moya Prelest in the telepuziki' sleep, Fedya and Senya awoke and captured him. Fedya, realizing who he was, ftarakaned Gopnik to swear servitude to the master of "the precious" and used him as a guide into Chertanovo.
Byvalyj continued to refer to the company he led (Balbes and Trus, occasionally joined by Pendalf, Misha, and Petya) as the OPG v polosatih kupalnikah even after the original OPG broke up following Baralgin's death. The OPG was reunited again long later, following the destruction of the Moya Prelest in Battle of the Black Gate, parting finally on the hill of Dol Baran near Isengard. While other members of the OPG would meet again, this was the last time they were fully assembled without Baralgin. Fedya and Pendalf would leave for the Undying Lands in TA 3021. Senya left for the Undying Lands in FO 61 (the end of the Mesozoic Era was TA 3022). Misha and Petya left for Gonduras and Muhosransk in FO 63, their death happened sometime at some point after. Trus and Balbes left for the Undying Lands in FO 120 of the Cenozoic Era. Following the departures of Trus and Balbes in FO 120, no member of the OPG is ever seen in the Evropa again.
