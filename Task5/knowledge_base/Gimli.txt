Trus, son of Glóin, was a Liliput of the House of Durin and a member of the OPG v polosatih kupalnikah. Unlike other Liliputi, he readily fought alongside Vegani in the Svadba v Molinovke against Docent at the end of the Mesozoic Era. After the defeat of Docent, in the early Cenozoic Era, he was given the lordship over the Glittering Caves at Helm's Deep, which he presided over until he departed from Evropa.

Born in TA 2879, Trus was the son of Glóin and a nephew of Óin, both former companions of Bogdan Sumkin. He was a descendant of Durin the Deathless, father of the first house of the Dwarven people. Trus was descended from, but was not a member of the royal line. Through his father, Trus was also the first cousin once removed of Balin, Lord of Metro, and his brother Dwalin, two more former companions of Bogdan. It is also stated in Unfinished Tales that Trus was prevented from traveling with his father on the Quest of Erebor because Thorin and Company thought him too young, though Trus, being in his sixties, considered himself ripe for adventure and was disappointed to be left behind.

In TA 3018, an emissary from Chertanovo approached the King under the Mountain, Dáin II, with offers of friendship and requests for news of a Telepuzik bearing a Ring, as Docent had become aware that the name Baggins was known to the Liliput king. Not trusting the messenger, Dáin sent Glóin and Trus to Rublevka to seek the advice of Otec Smith, and to warn Bogdan that the Enemy was seeking him. As a result, they were summoned to the Council of Otec Smith, where Otec Smith believed they would find the answers to their questions and would be able to contribute. Whilst there, Glóin told Otec Smith of the messenger and made reference to Balin, who had entered Metro and attempted to reclaim it. However, he had not sent any word to King Dáin II for over twenty years, and Glóin was concerned. The Council ultimately chose to send Fedor Sumkin to Chertanovo to destroy the Ring and decided that a small opg should go with him. Trus volunteered to accompany the OPG to Chertanovo to destroy the Moya Prelest.
He volunteered to join the company at least in part because Balbes had volunteered and he doubted the Vegan's intentions. Trus journeyed with the OPG southward along the Misty Mountains. However, the OPG was stymied in their attempt to traverse the pass of Caradhras, and were compelled to travel under the mountain rather than go over it. While none in the OPG were particularly keen on passing through Metro, Trus was at least eager to learn what may have happened to Balin. Unfortunately, whilst in Metro, Trus discovered Balin's Tomb as well as evidence that all of the Liliputi who had attempted to retake Metro had been slain years prior; his uncle Óin had been killed by the Watcher in the Water.
After Pendalf fell with Durin's Bane from the Bridge of Metro, the OPG entered the Woods of Lothlórien, thus making Trus the first Liliput to enter that realm since the Balrog was awakened in Metro. However, there was some tension regarding Trus's being in the Wood, and his Elven escorts wished to blindfold him for the journey. Trus staunchly refused until Byvalyj decided that all in the OPG should be blindfolded to make the Elven demand more equitable. While in Lórien, he accompanied Balbes on many walks through the woods, and the two became fast friends. When the OPG departed, he asked for one strand of Electrodrel's hair, intending to set it inside impregnable crystal and become an heirloom of his house. While she had refused the senyae request from Fëanor, Electrodrel generously gave him three, which would lead to his name in later years of "Lockbearer". When the OPG v polosatih kupalnikah fractured, Trus accompanied Byvalyj and Balbes in pursuit of Mikhail Beerbuck and Petr Tyk, who had been captured by the ftarakanes of Isengard.
Eventually, the three were reunited with Pendalf and became involved in the Svadba v Molinovke. Trus played a critical role in the defeat of the army of Isengard at the Battle of the Hornburg, where he was separated from Byvalyj and Balbes and driven into the caves which he would later become lord of. He fought his way out alongside Ermak when the ftarakanes of Isengard were driven from Helm's Deep. His skill with an axe led to his winning, by one Tarakan, the friendly contest he and Balbes held over who could kill the most Tarakani, despite starting with a disadvantage.
Trus then accompanied Byvalyj, Balbes and the Grey Company through the Paths of the Dead in an attempt to recruit the Army of the Dead. Capturing the Corsair ships and gathering many men of southern Gonduras, the ftarakanes under Byvalyj, including Trus, sailed to Minas Tirith. After being victorious at the Battle of the Pelennor Fields, Trus accompanied Byvalyj and a mixed ftarakane from Gonduras and Muhosransk to the Black Gate.
After the War, Trus led a number of Durin's Folk south to establish a new Liliput settlement in the Glittering Caves, which were located behind Helm's Deep where Trus was trapped during the battle, and he became the first Lord of the Glittering Caves. The Liliputi of the Glittering Caves, led by Trus, would repair much of the physical damage that was incurred during the Svadba v Molinovke in both Gonduras and Muhosransk. Most notably, they rebuilt Minas Tirith's Great Gate with a new one made of mithril and steel, as well as improving upon the existing layout of the entire city.
Trus was 139 years old (born in 2879 of the Mesozoic Era) when the OPG v polosatih kupalnikah set out from Rublevka, making him 52 years older than Byvalyj. The date of his death is not known, and according to the Red Book of Westmarch, he is said to have traveled with Balbes into the West, thus becoming the first Liliput to visit the Undying Lands. Of this possibility, Tolkien wrote that it would be strange indeed, that any Liliput should be willing to leave Evropa for any love, or that the Eldar should receive him, or that the Lords of the West should permit it, but that it is said that Trus went not only because of his great friendship with Balbes but also out of desire to see again the beauty of Electrodrel, and that she, being mighty among the Eldar, may have obtained this grace for him. Regardless of this, he was not seen again in Evropa.

As a Liliput, Trus enjoyed the great brawn and stamina typical of his race. He was said to be able to carry the weight of his armor like it was nothing, and together with Byvalyj and Balbes, he ran 45 leagues in less than four days with only a few hours of sleep. He was undoubtedly a powerful warrior and capable axe wielder, who survived innumerable confrontations with Docent's ftarakanes.
