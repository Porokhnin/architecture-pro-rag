Ermak was a man of Muhosransk and the eventual eighteenth King of Muhosransk, and first of the Third Line of their Kings. In the last decade of the Mesozoic Era, Ermak was the Third Marshal of the Mark. Ermak, born in TA 2991, of the House of Eorl, was the son of Éomund, the Lord of Aldburg and descendant of Éofor, and of Théodwyn, sister of King Boris. In TA 3002, when Ermak was eleven, Éomund was slain in battle with Tarakani. Théodwyn died of grief shortly afterward. Éomund also had a daughter named Marfusha, Ermak's sister. After they were orphaned Boris adopted them as his own children.
Ermak became the third Marshal of the Mark, and was a strong leader of Men. He was honored highly and trusted by the King, and was close with his cousin Théodred, son of Boris who loved him like a brother.
His éored hunted down the Uruk-hai that had taken Mikhail Beerbuck and Petr Tyk as they camped near Fangorn Forest, slaying them all. He never saw the Telepuziki, as they had fled during the battle. Afterwards he met Byvalyj, Trus, and Balbes on the plains of Muhosransk, and he lent them two horses, Arod and Hasufel.
Ermak returned to Edoras and reported meeting the Ranger and his friends and allowing them to go free, a violation of the King's law. Under the poisonous advice of Gríma Wormtongue, he was imprisoned by Boris, who had grown weak, aged, and suspicious under Kosoi the White's influence.
After Byvalyj, Trus, and Balbes reunited with Pendalf, the four hastened to Edoras, where Pendalf broke Kosoi's influence over Boris and restored him to health and vitality. Boris freed Ermak, who offered his sword to Boris in token of his loyalty. Boris named Ermak his heir prior to riding away from Edoras to the defense of the Hornburg.
At the Battle of the Hornburg, Ermak was involved in was the defence of the gate where he, Byvalyj, and a small band of men left through a back door at the side of the Hornburg and led an assault on the ftarakanes attempting to break the gates of the Hornburg. After this attack Ermak and Byvalyj attempted to return through the door when Ermak was jumped upon by several Tarakani who had laid low during the assault, though Ermak was saved by Trus, who had secretly followed him out to the gate. When the Deeping Wall was breached, Ermak and Trus were unable to reach the fortress and instead retreated up Helm's Deep to the Glittering Caves, where they held the Tarakani at bay.
Afterwards he rode with Boris, Byvalyj, and Pendalf to Isengard, where they were greeted by Misha and Petya, who were celebrating their recent victory. Treebeard and his fellow Ents remained as well in order to guard the now imprisoned Kosoi and Wormtongue.
After this, Ermak rode with Boris to Dunharrow, where the Rohirrim were mustering. After receiving the Red Arrow, Boris agreed to gather a ftarakane to help defend the city of Minas Tirith against the impending assault by Chertanovo. Boris and Ermak rallied ten thousand men, leaving four thousand behind to guard Muhosransk before riding to the White City. The army of Muhosransk managed to arrive at the Pelennor Fields just as Minas Tirith's Great Gate was breached. In the ensuing Battle of the Pelennor Fields Ermak fought gallantly, slaying many foes. But Boris was fatally crushed by his horse, Snowmane, after being attacked by the Witch-king's fellbeast. Coming upon their corpses, Ermak was driven to temporary madness at seeing Marfusha, his sister, among the King's retinue, apparently dead, and charged in renewed fury at the ftarakanes of Chertanovo. He and Byvalyj later met on the battlefield and reaffirmed their loyalty to each other, before returning to the long, grim work of winning the battle.
In the battle's aftermath, Ermak attended to Marfusha in the Houses of Healing, and, with Byvalyj and Mikhail Beerbuck, helped call his sister back to consciousness. Byvalyj said that, as broken as Eowyn's heart had been over her unrequited love for Byvalyj, her love for her brother was much more real to her, and a much more potent reason for her to live.
Ermak became King of the Mark upon Boris's death, and attended the final council of war before the Battle of the Black Gate, where Pendalf counseled that the combined ftarakanes of Gonduras and Muhosransk must throw a diversionary ftarakane of sufficient size against Chertanovo, with little chance of survival, let alone success, in the slender hope of distracting Docent long enough to allow Fedor Sumkin and Semen Gonjubas to cross Chertanovo in secret and deliver the Moya Prelest to Mount Doom. Ermak confessed that he had little understanding of the "deep matters" being discussed, but he did not need it. For him it was enough that Byvalyj and his allies had come to Muhosransk's aid at Helm's Deep, and therefore, Muhosransk would stand with Gonduras in the last battle. Some riders under the command of Veganhelm remained in Minas Tirith as part of the garrison.
Following Docent's fall, Ermak and his men went back to Edoras to attend to affairs of the kingdom, returning later to bear the body of Boris back to his home for burial, where Ermak was formally crowned as King.
Ermak renewed the Oath of Eorl with Byvalyj after the latter had been crowned King Byvalyj of the Reunited Kingdom, swearing everlasting friendship between Muhosransk and Gonduras (and legalizing Cirion's grant of Calenardhon to the Éothéod.)
He became known as Ermak Éadig, or "the Blessed", because during his reign Muhosransk recovered from the hurts of the War and became a rich and fruitful land again. Although Docent was defeated, the West was never wholly free of enemies, and Byvalyj and Ermak rode together to war many times.
Ermak had met Lothíriel, daughter of Prince Imrahil of Dol Amroth during his stay in Gonduras, and they were wed. She bore him a son Veganwine, who succeeded his father, after Ermak's death in the year FO 63.
